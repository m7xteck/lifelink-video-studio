## Summary (for orientation only; the JSON wins on any difference)
- **Look:** paper-and-ink cut paper. Paper ground, oxblood ink, royal/sky accents, rose for tiny accents.
  Palette tokens only, never hard-coded colours.
- **Type:** LifeLink Text for everything readable; LifeLink Script only for short accents (≤4 words, ≥56 px,
  never all-caps, no word starting with capital I — it reads as J, no numerals, avoid "x").
- **Motion:** soft-start easing (nothing goes from rest to full speed in one frame), entering travel ≤ ~160 px,
  no rotating flat cards/text, camera drift ≤ 1.02, text holds ≥ 1.5 s, still once settled, no bouncy/elastic motion.
- **Motifs (all PROPOSED):** twoPaths (once per lesson at most), blindEmboss, paperCut illustrations.
  Rejected: two-ring motif (see `../rejected.md`).
- **Imagery/values:** no hearts, wedding rings or ring-like pairs, crescents, generic Islamic patterns, people,
  faces, hands, gamified UI, percent-match graphics, dating-app language. Warm, sincere, never preachy.
- **Watermark:** mandatory while status ≠ APPROVED.

The client approved the concepts v1 *direction* for the L01 preview (2026-11-23) — that is a creative go-ahead,
not a brand lock. Open items are listed in `LOCKED.md`.
