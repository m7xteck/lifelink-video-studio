# Rejected treatments

What was tried and rejected, why, when and by whom. Never reintroduce one of these without the client asking.
Sources: `decisions.md`, `lesson-notes/lifelink-course-L01.md`, `experiments/brand-concepts-v1.md`,
`experiments/variety-v2.md`, `brand/design-system.json` (motifs.rejected), `brand/proposals/interpretation-1.0.md`.

| # | Date | Treatment | Rejected by | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | 2026-11-23 | Two-ring / link-rings motif (brand proposal.1) | Independent review (before client saw it); prohibition in questionnaire | Read as wedding bands/rings; romantic cliché | Removed. No ring-like pairs anywhere; twoPaths motif proposed instead |
| R2 | 2026-11-23 | Script capital "I", script numerals, words with "x" in LifeLink Script | Independent brand review | Capital I reads as J; numerals and "x" illegible in the script face | Script only for ≤4-word accents without capital I/numerals; avoid "x"; numbers in the text face |
| R3 | 2026-11-23 | Opening card: ~900 px slide starting at full speed (18% of travel in frame 1) | Client: "a bit choppy" | Jolting entrance, judder at 30 fps | 120 px eased rise + fade; global entrance curve softened (2.6% in frame 1); travel ≤ ~160 px |
| R4 | 2026-11-23 | Type-only preview (v003 of the 40 s preview) | Client: "needs to be more graphical or pictorial and have elements" | Felt like slides | Paper-cut illustration system (Layer/Ink) where meaningful |
| R5 | 2026-11-23 | PathLandscape v1: stepping stones at hand-estimated coordinates, hills drifting independently | Client: "the steps arent even on the path" | Stones visibly off the path; unlinked motion | Path from one computed centre-line (fold-free search), stones placed ON it, hill drift removed |
| R6 | 2026-11-23 | Sunrise half hidden behind the hills | Client: "the sunrise is cropped out" | Key image cropped | Sun clipped to sky, rises fully clear on "relationships of your life" |
| R7 | 2026-11-23 | Decorative motion: hill drift/rise, moving pen, second light sweep | Client: animations "extremely random or unlinked" | Motion not tied to narration | All decorative motion removed; still once settled |
| R8 | 2026-11-23 | ConvergingPaths — three lines (three kinds of learner) to one door, s003 | Client: "looks cheap and extra with no meaning" | Literal, cheap-looking, no meaning added | Reverted to text `parallelPhrases`; s001–s004 frozen |
| R9 | 2026-11-24 | L01 v003 full lesson: same rising "drop-down" text with small tracked kicker + rule on most scenes (kineticStatement 119 + parallelPhrases 55 = 25 of 36 min; 138 kickers) | Client: "every single part feels repeated … all the same drop down style text with the line above" | Monotony; no purposeful animation; not memorable | Signature image per chapter, text reveals rise/focus/ink, kicker only at chapter openers, rhythm.ts |

Generic avoid-list from the client (2026-11-24): generic infographics, childish icons, crowded diagrams, decorative
motion, AI-looking design, repeated fades/scales/slides/static diagrams.
