# Art direction notes on v2 signature components (Executive Director review of stills, 2026-11-24 08:20)
Verdict: the concepts are right and readable; the execution is "good infographic", not yet premium studio. Upgrade before v004 render.
- threePillars: base slabs are heavy flat oxblood bars; roof is a thin outline triangle (clip-art feel); columns are generic.
  → cut-paper construction with real depth (layered sheets, soft paper shadows, PaperGround texture on shapes), refined proportions,
    lighter base, roof as a folded paper pediment; the column rising should feel built, not faded in. Labels: script word + meaning OK.
  → At 290 s ("the first is sakina") only an empty base shows: the first column must be visibly rising as the word is spoken.
- ecosystemRings: reads as a generic target/bullseye with tiny curved labels ("lawful intimacy" illegible).
  → layered paper discs/sheets slightly offset (depth), fewer, larger labels set horizontally beside the ring they name with fine leader rules;
    the active ring lifts (shadow) as it is discussed; keep faith at the centre.
- iceberg: best of the three. Tip is small relative to the frame; "what others see" label faint. Water plane is flat colour.
  → slightly larger tip, subtle waterline shimmer only when the hidden mass is revealed (meaningful), paper texture on ice.
General: fewer, larger elements; generous negative space; paper-and-ink materiality (texture, layered shadows) on every signature;
motion = construction (assemble, unfold, lift) rather than opacity fades.
