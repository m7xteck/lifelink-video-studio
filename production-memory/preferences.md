_None confirmed by the client yet._

Direction given in the studio brief (2026-11-23): premium hybrid + intelligent adaptive storytelling; refined, intentional, emotionally intelligent; no slideshow look; no gratuitous motion.

## Confirmed by client (2026-11-23)
- Motion must be MEANINGFUL: every moving element maps to something the narration says; no decorative/random motion. Settled frames are still.
- Prefer pictorial/illustrated scenes with relevant elements over type-only slides.
- Smoothness matters: no choppy/jolting movement.

## Client direction 2026-11-24 — premium standard (standing rules, client's own words summarised)
- Every scene must clearly support the actual narration; never animate just to fill time.
- Each chapter: its own distinct visual concept, fully within the approved branding.
- Sophisticated motion: progressive construction, meaningful transformations, layered movement, intelligent metaphors, sequences that develop with the explanation — not repeated fades/scales/slides/static diagrams.
- Visual memory: establish a recognisable image per concept, bring it back and develop it when the concept returns, connect ideas in visual recaps — intentional, not repetitive.
- Adaptive pacing: no fixed percentage quotas; rich vs restrained by the narration's needs; no consecutive identical animation patterns.
- Premium art direction: no generic infographics, childish icons, crowded diagrams, decorative motion, or AI-looking design.
- QC on rendered video clips (entrances/exits, timing, rhythm, smoothness, narration sync, chapter transitions) across the full length, not stills only.
- Dashboard: separate measured activity from estimates; show verified executions, renders, QA findings and blockers; never present estimates or simulation as real.
- Independent learner-perspective creative audit before delivery, prioritised by viewer impact.
- Preserve working brand, compositions, animations and files; revise only where justified; keep version history; no regressions.
