# LifeLink brand intake

Nothing here has been decided yet. The current look is a neutral test palette and is
watermarked on every render. Send whatever you have. Partial answers are fine, and
"not sure" is a useful answer too.

## 1. Files to upload (drop them in the chat; they'll be stored in `brand/intake/uploads/`)
- [ ] Logo: ideally SVG, otherwise high-resolution PNG with transparent background. Include variations (full, icon-only, light/dark) if they exist.
- [ ] Brand guidelines PDF, if one exists.
- [ ] Font files (.otf/.ttf/.woff2) or the font names. If they're commercial fonts, confirm LifeLink holds a licence that covers video use.
- [ ] Colour palette (hex codes), or a screenshot of the website/app.
- [ ] 2–5 visual references you love (videos, websites, brands). Say what you like about each.
- [ ] 1–3 references you dislike, and why.

## 2. Personality: pick 3–5 words
Warm · calm · hopeful · elegant · modern · traditional · playful · serious · intimate · authoritative · gentle · bold · spiritual · practical · premium · friendly · other: ____

## 3. Audience & values
- Who are the learners? (age range, stage of life, regions/cultures)
- Is there a faith or cultural framework the visuals should respect or reflect? (If so, which one, and what does that mean visually?)
- Imagery rules: are human figures allowed? Couples? Physical contact? Modesty guidelines? Should illustrations be abstract only?
- Topics that need particular sensitivity?

## 4. Sound
- Background music: yes / no / only in intros and outros? Any restrictions (e.g. no instruments)?
- Subtle sound accents on transitions: yes / no?
- Is there licensed music LifeLink already owns?

## 5. Video details
- Intro/outro: do you want a branded opening sting and closing card? What should the closing card say (website, call to action)?
- Burned-in subtitles on the video, or only a separate subtitle file (SRT)?
- Course thumbnail style preferences?

## What happens next
The brand-guardian turns this into a proposed design system, with sample frames
rendered by the real engine using your brand. You review it, request changes, and
approve it. Only then is it locked as the LifeLink design system, and the watermark
comes off.
