# LifeLink video brand: interpretation & proposal 1.0

Status: **PROPOSAL, awaiting client approval.** Nothing here is locked.
Built from: `brand/intake/BRAND_INTAKE.md` (questionnaire + artboard + fonts).
Proposed tokens: `brand/design-system.json` (version 1.0.0-proposal.1).

## The idea in one line
**"Paper and ink."** LifeLink videos should feel like beautifully made stationery that comes
to life: considered, tactile and quiet. Think a letter from someone you trust, not an app advert.
This is drawn from your references (embossed card folder, editorial wedding stationery)
and your admired brands (Aesop restraint, Apple clarity), and it supports your three words:
- **Trustworthy:** calm, steady motion; nothing flashy; text always readable.
- **Intentional:** every element arrives when it is spoken; nothing moves just to move.
- **Refined:** paper texture, emboss, hairlines and a few colours used with discipline.

## Colour: how the palette is used (provisional hex values)
| Role | Colour | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | Cream `#F3F0E8` | Default background: paper |
| Ink | Oxblood `#500002` | Headlines and body text on cream; also a full-bleed ground for reflective moments |
| Field | Royal blue `#3961BC` | Bold card-stock fields (introductions, summary spine); secondary line in diagrams; script accents on cream |
| Accent | Light blue `#9BB9D6` | Shared/connected space in diagrams; script on oxblood |
| Highlight | Muted red `#AF3E46` | Tiny accents only: numerals, the active step, short rules |
| Black | `#060606` | Held in reserve (not used in these concepts) |
Derived shades (deeper oxblood/blue for vignettes, a muted oxblood for secondary text,
a hairline tone) are listed separately in the design system and need your approval too.
**Not used:** the heart red `#D44455`.

## Typography: two faces, clear jobs
- **LifeLink Script** (Australian Mirages): the wordmark and *short accent words only*, e.g.
  "before", "the right person", "Introduction", "In summary". Never long sentences or all-caps (it
  becomes unreadable).
- **LifeLink Text** (the supplied "Geneva"): everything else, from huge headlines to labels.
  It has one weight, so hierarchy comes from size, colour and tracked capitals.
- Two technical corrections (display only, font files unchanged except one compatibility rebuild):
  the body font's word spaces are unusually wide and its line metrics oversized; both are corrected
  optically. See `public/fonts/lifelink-provisional/README.md`.

## Motion language
- Headline letters **rise like set type**; script **appears like ink**; cards **slide and settle
  like paper**; hairlines **draw**; the embossed rings are **revealed by a raking light**.
- No bounce, zoom, spin, particles, glow or "tech" effects. Very slow drift at most.
- Reflective moments get longer, softer transitions; teaching moments are crisper.

## Motifs (proposed)
- **Two paths meeting:** two lines (two people) travel separately and join into one two-tone line,
  each remaining whole. Used once per lesson at most, where the content is a process that ends in an
  introduction. It visualises your belief without romantic imagery.
- **Blind emboss:** the wordmark pressed into blue card stock, revealed by a raking light (from your
  ORRAV folder reference).
- **Rejected before presenting:** an earlier two-ring motif. Independent review found it read as
  wedding bands (a cliché you asked to avoid), so it was removed. Your actual mark (a solid disc with
  an offset counter-form) is not redrawn or imitated anywhere.
