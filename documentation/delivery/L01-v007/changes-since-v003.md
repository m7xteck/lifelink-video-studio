# What changed from v003 to v007

## The big picture

| | v003 | v007 |
|---|---|---|
| Scenes | 251 | 149, with fewer cuts and longer pictures that build as you speak |
| Different scene designs | 11 | 27 |
| "Drop-down text with a line above" scenes (`kineticStatement`) | 119, about half of all scenes | 23, now using 3 different entrances: rise, focus and ink |
| Time on signature pictures | almost none | 60% of the lesson (21.6 min) |
| Fully still for more than 8 s | not measurable then | 12 stretches, about 2 min |
| Loudness | −26.1 LUFS, too quiet | −16.5 LUFS (standard for online video) |
| Brand | provisional, watermarked "provisional brand" | locked: your exact colours, fonts and real logo |
| Automated checks | 1 fail | all pass |

## The changes that matter most

1. **One signature picture per chapter**, built step by step as each idea is spoken, and returned to when the idea comes back:
   - three pillars (Sakina, Mawaddah, Rahmah);
   - ecosystem rings around faith;
   - iceberg;
   - pressures and shelter;
   - sorting trays (Islamic teachings vs cultural expectations);
   - bridge of planks;
   - balance scale (responsibility vs control);
   - foundation layers;
   - scenario case files;
   - myth/truth tear-away cards;
   - the mirror check;
   - a lesson map between chapters.
2. **New text treatments:**
   - highlighted line;
   - scripture pull-quote with attribution;
   - question prompt;
   - word stack;
   - kinetic statements with 3 entrance styles instead of one.
3. **Pacing follows the narration, not quotas.** Pictures stay on screen while you develop an idea, instead of cutting away and back.
4. **Continuity:**
   - pictures keep earlier steps visible, shown faint, so viewers remember where they are;
   - chapter recaps bring back the images from each chapter;
   - the lesson ends with a recap of 5–6 images.
5. **Review fixes from the v004 video review and learner audit**, which were done on real clips:
   - the bridge is now one continuous scene;
   - the balance scale stays on screen through "control";
   - dead-air gaps removed;
   - readable highlight colours;
   - no script lettering for "not" or "never";
   - the repair example folded into the foundation picture;
   - the iceberg waterline;
   - caption gaps filled.
6. **No frozen holds (v007).** A slow documentary push-in runs on every scene, and the balance scale inks *not ownership* as you say it.
7. **Brand locked:** your exact colour codes, Australian Mirages and Geneva fonts, and the real logo in the opening and closing.
8. **Audio:** the gentle limiter you approved, bringing loudness to the online standard. The narration itself is untouched.
