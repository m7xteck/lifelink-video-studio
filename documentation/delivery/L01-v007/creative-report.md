# Creative improvement report: Module 01, Lesson 01 (v003 → v007)

## What you asked for
After v003 you said every part felt repeated ("all the same drop down style text with the line above") and there wasn't enough purposeful animation. A viewer should be able to watch all 36 minutes without getting bored, understand what is said, and remember it.

## What we did

**1. A picture for each big idea, built as you speak.** Each chapter now has its own signature image, assembled piece by piece on the words that name each piece:

| Image | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Three pillars | Sakina, Mawaddah, Rahmah |
| Ecosystem rings | Companionship, support, faith and character, shared responsibility and family, all around faith |
| Iceberg | What others see, and the character beneath |
| Pressures and shelter | Pressures on a marriage, and what shelters it |
| Sorting trays | Islamic teachings vs cultural expectations |
| Bridge of planks | The four elements of working together |
| Balance scale | Responsibility vs control |
| Foundation layers | Qualities of marriage |
| Scenario case files | Three practical scenarios |
| Myth/truth cards | Common myths |
| Mirror check | Self-reflection questions |

These pictures fill 60% of the lesson.

**2. Memory and continuity.**
- Pictures come back when the idea returns.
- Earlier steps stay visible but faint, so the viewer knows where they are.
- A lesson map appears between chapters.
- Chapter recaps bring the images back, and the lesson ends with a "learning journey" grid of them.

**3. Variety in the text moments.**
- There are 27 scene designs, up from 11.
- The drop-down text style is down from 119 scenes to 23, and those 23 now enter in three different ways: rise, focus and ink.
- New treatments: highlighted line, scripture quote with attribution, question prompt and word stack.

**4. Pacing follows the narration.**
- No fixed quotas.
- The lesson went from 251 cuts to 149 scenes, so pictures stay while you develop an idea.
- Dead-air gaps were found on real clips and removed.

**5. Nothing sits frozen.**
- A slow documentary push-in runs on every scene. You approved this as a brand exception on 2026-11-25.
- Frozen time is down from 9.6 min to 2 min.

**6. Your brand, locked.** Exact colours, your fonts and your real logo in the opening and closing.

## Evidence that it works

**Independent reviews on real footage**
- v004: 3 Quality Directors and a learner auditor reviewed real clips. All of their high and medium findings were fixed in v005.
- v007: an independent reviewer, who did not build the video, gave the verdict **"ready for client review"**. They singled out:
  - the balance scale as the strongest stretch;
  - the scenario cards as matching the narration line for line;
  - the careful handling of religious sources and sensitive topics.

**Measured, not judged:** scene counts, design variety, frozen time and loudness are all taken from the files. The technical QA report has the details.
